# Bariatric surgery for co-morbid conditions related to morbid obesity

Source: Medicare NCD 100.1 (version effective 09/24/2013).
https://www.cms.gov/medicare-coverage-database/details/ncd-details.aspx?NCDId=57

Covered when all of the following are met.

- **bar_bmi.** Body mass index (BMI) of 35 or more. The most recent measurement applies.
- **bar_comorbid.** At least one co-morbidity related to obesity (for example type 2 diabetes,
  hypertension, obstructive sleep apnea, dyslipidemia, osteoarthritis, fatty liver disease).
- **bar_prior.** The beneficiary has previously been unsuccessful with medical treatment for
  obesity (for example a supervised weight-loss program or weight-loss medication).
- **bar_procedure.** The requested procedure is a covered type: open or laparoscopic Roux-en-Y
  gastric bypass, open or laparoscopic biliopancreatic diversion with duodenal switch,
  laparoscopic adjustable gastric banding, or laparoscopic sleeve gastrectomy.

Not covered: treatment of obesity alone; open adjustable gastric banding; open sleeve
gastrectomy; vertical banded gastroplasty; intestinal bypass; gastric balloon.
