# Home oxygen therapy: initial coverage

Sources: Medicare NCD 240.2, Home Use of Oxygen (version effective 09/27/2021),
https://www.cms.gov/medicare-coverage-database/details/ncd-details.aspx?NCDId=169 ; testing
window from the DME MAC LCD L33797, Oxygen and Oxygen Equipment.

Home oxygen is covered when all of the following are met.

- **o2_test.** A qualifying test of arterial oxygen at rest, on room air: arterial PaO2 of
  55 mm Hg or less, or oxygen saturation (SpO2) of 88% or less (Group I). A result of PaO2
  56 to 59 mm Hg or SpO2 of 89% qualifies only together with **o2_group2** (Group II).
- **o2_group2.** Applies only to Group II results. The record documents at least one of:
  dependent edema suggesting congestive heart failure; pulmonary hypertension or cor pulmonale;
  or erythrocythemia with hematocrit above 56%.
- **o2_timing.** The qualifying test was performed within 30 days before the order.
- **o2_tester.** The test was ordered and evaluated by the treating practitioner and performed
  by a qualified provider. A DME supplier is not a qualified provider for this test. Judge this
  from who performed the qualifying test, as recorded on the test itself.

Not covered: breathlessness without hypoxemia or cor pulmonale; angina without hypoxemia.
