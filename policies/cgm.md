# Continuous glucose monitor (CGM)

Source: Medicare LCD L33822, Glucose Monitors (revision effective 10/01/2024).
https://www.cms.gov/medicare-coverage-database/view/lcd.aspx?lcdid=33822

A CGM is covered when all of the following are met.

- **cgm_dx.** The beneficiary has diabetes mellitus.
- **cgm_need.** Either the beneficiary is treated with insulin, or has problematic
  hypoglycemia: recurrent level 2 events (glucose below 54 mg/dL) that persist despite more than
  one attempt to adjust medication or the treatment plan, or at least one level 3 event (altered
  mental or physical state requiring third-party assistance).
- **cgm_visit.** An in-person or approved telehealth visit with the treating practitioner,
  evaluating diabetes control, within 6 months before the order.
