# Positive airway pressure (CPAP, HCPCS E0601) for obstructive sleep apnea: initial coverage

Source: Medicare LCD L33718, Positive Airway Pressure (PAP) Devices for the Treatment of
Obstructive Sleep Apnea (DME MACs; revision effective 01/01/2024), consistent with NCD 240.4.
https://www.cms.gov/medicare-coverage-database/view/lcd.aspx?lcdid=33718

A single-level CPAP device is covered for an initial 12-week period when all of the following
are met.

- **cpap_eval.** The beneficiary had an in-person clinical evaluation by the treating
  practitioner before the sleep test.
- **cpap_test.** A Medicare-qualifying sleep test (facility polysomnogram or a home sleep test)
  shows either an apnea-hypopnea index (AHI) or respiratory disturbance index (RDI) of 15 or more
  events per hour with at least 30 events, or an AHI or RDI of 5 to 14 events per hour with at
  least 10 events.
- **cpap_symptoms.** Applies only when the AHI or RDI is 5 to 14. The record documents at least
  one of: excessive daytime sleepiness, impaired cognition, mood disorder, insomnia, hypertension,
  ischemic heart disease, or history of stroke.
- **cpap_education.** The beneficiary or caregiver received instruction from the supplier on the
  proper use and care of the equipment.

Claims that do not meet all criteria are denied as not reasonable and necessary.
