# Implantable cardioverter defibrillator (ICD) for primary prevention in dilated cardiomyopathy

Source: Medicare NCD 20.4, Implantable Cardioverter Defibrillators (indications effective
02/15/2018). https://www.cms.gov/medicare-coverage-database/details/ncd-details.aspx?NCDId=110

Covered for severe ischemic or non-ischemic dilated cardiomyopathy without prior sustained
ventricular tachycardia or ventricular fibrillation arrest when all of the following are met.

- **icd_indication.** Dilated cardiomyopathy with heart failure of NYHA class II or III and left
  ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF) of 35% or less. The most recent LVEF measurement applies.
- **icd_gdmt.** Applies only to non-ischemic cardiomyopathy. At least 3 months (90 days) of
  optimal medical therapy before the order.
  <!-- check: at_least_days=90 -->
- **icd_sdm.** A formal shared decision-making encounter, using an evidence-based decision tool,
  took place before the order.
- **icd_exclusions.** None of: myocardial infarction within the past 40 days; coronary artery
  bypass graft or percutaneous coronary intervention within the past 3 months (90 days);
  NYHA class IV heart failure; cardiogenic shock. Met when the record documents the current heart
  failure class and contains none of these events in those windows; not met when it contains one.
